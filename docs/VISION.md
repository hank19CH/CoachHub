# CoachHub — Vision

> Working name (Ascend under consideration). Tagline: **Reach Your Summit.**
> Source of truth for product direction. Last updated 2026-11-03.

## One line

**The social home for athletic life** — where training, testing and sport become the content. Coaches run their whole business on it, athletes live in it daily, and everyone can see how they measure up — against their friends, their region, and the pros.

## Where it started

> "I was testing athletes at a rugby academy and uploading the results to their profiles for selectors to review. Then I thought — why isn't this a leaderboard? Why isn't this social? Why can't anyone see how they measure up against a pro?"

Founder: 20+ years elite coaching · Australian National Team Sprints Coach.

Athletic performance data is social, competitive and aspirational — yet it lives in spreadsheets, locked enterprise platforms and private club systems. CoachHub gives it a home.

## The opportunity — two markets, one missing bridge

| Layer | Who | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Social | Instagram, Strava | Shallow, unverified — a feed with nothing real underneath |
| Performance | BridgeAthletic, Teamworks, Smartabase, TrainingPeaks | Rich real data — enterprise-priced, walled off, anti-social |

**CoachHub fuses them:** real coaching and verified testing data become native social content.

## Go-to-market — the coach is the wedge

A coach assigns a workout → the athlete opens the app → completes it → it becomes a feed post → a streak ticks, a PB fires. A daily habit exists before virality has to. Each coach brings 15–20 athletes; 50 coaches seed an engaged graph producing real data.

## The product — five pillars

### 1. Coach business OS
Coaches run their **entire business** here. Personal trainers and coaches sell anything from one storefront:

| Offer | Athlete gets | Coach effort |
|---|---|---|
| Program only | A ready-made block, bought once | Build once, sell many |
| Virtual coaching | Personalised plan, check-ins, adjustments | Ongoing, remote |
| In-person squad / training group | Group sessions, attendance, shared plan | Roster, not spreadsheets |
| Elite 1:1 | Fully data-driven plan | High-touch |

### 2. Plan once, scale everywhere
Build a canonical week → the Block Modeler shapes the block (progression, deloads, levers, pins). Deterministic: the coach stays in control. The same engine serves a $20 program and an Olympian.

### 3. Every sport
Strength, track, swimming, running, triathlon, team sports. Sessions in sets × reps **or** distance / pace / zones / time.

### 4. Data-informed coaching (later)
Health and device data (HRV, sleep, load, HR zones, pace, power — Garmin, Strava, Whoop, Oura, Apple Health) flows into the coach's view and informs the next week. Readiness flags: push or hold. The coach decides; the data informs.

### 5. Verified testing, leaderboards & the pros
The original insight, and the moat.

- **Pro partnerships:** professional teams and organisations share testing data → everyday people compare themselves to pros, and regionally.
- **Verification tiers** on every result: *Self-reported → Coach-logged → Lab-verified (CoachHub testing centre / certified partner) → Pro partner.* Leaderboards filter by tier.
- **Protocols are the moat:** every test has a fixed protocol (method, equipment, units, scoring). Hand-timed ≠ laser-timed. Standardised protocols make "you vs a pro" fair, and partners adopting them is hard to copy.
- **Leaderboard cuts:** test · sport/position · age band · sex · bodyweight class where relevant · region (club → city → state → country) · verification tier.
- **Privacy & consent:** partner data-sharing agreements (named / anonymised / averages); minors need guardian consent and are not publicly ranked by default; biometrics treated as health data.

### 6. CoachHub Testing Centres (physical)
Brick-and-mortar testing labs (e.g. in shopping centres) where **anyone** can walk in and get tested — VO2 max, force-plate jump (Sparta-style), 20-yard dash, body composition, and more. Every result lands in their CoachHub profile as **lab-verified**.

Why it matters: verified data supply, mass-market acquisition (people who don't have a coach), and revenue independent of subscriptions. De-risk in stages:
1. **Pop-up / mobile testing days** at clubs, schools and events
2. **Certified partner sites** (gyms, physio clinics) running CoachHub protocols
3. **Owned flagship centre** once demand is proven

## The plan — deliberate layers

| # | Layer | Status | Revenue / effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Foundation — coaching tool + social habit loop | Now (~90% built) | Coach subscriptions (Coach $19 beta / $29, Team $59 / $79). No athlete fees |
| 1 | Verified testing + leaderboards (coach-logged) | Next | Content + retention; sets up the moat |
| 2 | Coach business OS — storefront, virtual coaching, squads | Next | Platform fee on coach sales |
| 3 | Multi-sport + device data | Later | Endurance/elite market; data-informed coaching |
| 4 | Pro partnerships + Testing Centres | Later | Mass-market acquisition; testing revenue |
| 5 | Creator economy + The Locker (kit, affiliate) | Later | Creator subs + affiliate |
| 6 | Marketplace + club/brand accounts | Later | Transaction & marketplace fees |

"No athlete fees" means the platform never charges athletes to be on it; athletes pay coaches for programs/coaching, and the platform takes a share.

## Why it's defensible
- Verified data tied to a live social graph
- Standardised testing protocols adopted by partners and centres
- The coach relationship (coaches bring athletes and reputation; low churn)
- Pro partnerships as content nobody else has
- Sequencing discipline — each layer stands on real revenue and audience
